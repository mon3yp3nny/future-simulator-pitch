Shaping the future

Ten static frames for the intro animation, with timings, the exact text that appears on screen and the optional narration. The narration can be switched on and off at any time with the M key or the speaker symbol in the bottom-right corner. The captions carry the whole message, so nothing depends on the sound.

  • 73 s runtime
  • 10 scenes
  • 16:9 legible at 1280×720
  • Voice on or off
  • English

Where this differs from the prompt

  • The sample question is “Window, middle or aisle?”, with three answers like the real questionnaire. It fits the flight and says nothing about AI.
  • Scene 3 has a caption the prompt did not list, so the map makes sense without a presenter.
  • Scene 7 has a caption, and each waypoint shows the size of its turn in degrees, the way a heading change is given in navigation.
  • The aircraft is drawn to the proportions of an Airbus A350 seen from above, with its curved wingtips. It is an own drawing with no logo or livery.
  • The two score axes are shown as one thing: turning left or right. It explains the principle but is not the real scoring model.
  • Sound is optional. There is one spoken line per scene, shown under Voice. The speaker symbol is always visible on the start screen; while the animation plays it appears only when the mouse moves.
  • All text is set in B612, the open-source typeface made for aircraft cockpit displays. It stays readable when a screen share is compressed.
